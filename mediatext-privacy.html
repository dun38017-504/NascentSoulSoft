MediaText Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Overview

MediaText converts audio and video selected by you into text. We designed the app to minimize data collection. We do not operate an account system, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK in MediaText.

Files and transcripts

Selected media is copied to temporary storage on your device for processing. Video audio is extracted locally. Temporary working files are removed after the transcription attempt. Completed transcript history is stored locally on your device. We do not upload your files or transcripts to servers operated by us.

Apple services

MediaText uses Apple Speech Recognition. Depending on your device, language, settings, and Apple service availability, audio may be processed by Apple. Apple’s handling of that data is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and system terms.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We receive transaction and entitlement information needed to unlock Pro, but we do not receive your full payment-card details.

Permissions

The app requests access only when needed to select media and use speech recognition. You may change permissions in iOS Settings.

Children

MediaText is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. Questions may be sent to dun38017@gmail.com.

MediaText 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 20 日

概述

MediaText 将您主动选择的音频或视频转换为文字。我们以尽量少收集数据为原则，未在 MediaText 中设置账户系统、广告 SDK 或分析 SDK。

文件与转写结果

您选择的媒体会被复制到设备临时目录进行处理，视频音轨在本地提取。转写尝试结束后，临时工作文件会被删除。完成的转写历史保存在您的设备本地。我们不会将您的文件或转写内容上传至我们运营的服务器。

Apple 服务

MediaText 使用 Apple 语音识别。根据设备、语言、系统设置和 Apple 服务可用性，音频可能由 Apple 处理，相关数据处理受 Apple 隐私政策与系统条款约束。

购买

App 内购买由 Apple 处理。我们会获取解锁 Pro 所需的交易及权益状态，但不会取得您的完整银行卡信息。

权限

App 仅在选择媒体和使用语音识别时请求必要权限。您可以在 iOS“设置”中修改授权。

儿童

MediaText 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童个人信息。

变更与联系

我们可能随功能变化更新本政策。如有问题，请联系 dun38017@gmail.com。
