帮助与支持

适用于成语小玩家

如何开始猜题？

点击底部“猜猜”，观察图片并从四个成语中选择答案。工具版没有倒计时，答错后可以继续选择，也可以查看答案。

为什么只能看到10题？

前10题免费体验。你可以通过非消耗型购买永久解锁剩余90题，并在同一 Apple 账号下恢复购买。

如何下载图片？

点击猜题页右上角保存图标，或在历史记录中勾选多张图片后保存。“图片下载”永久版还支持每次下载5张图片、配套文案和视频制作。

音乐没有声音怎么办？

请确认“我的—背景音乐”已打开，并调高设备媒体音量。若刚从后台返回，可重新进入页面或切换一次背景音乐开关。

如何恢复购买？

在购买窗口点击“恢复购买”，并确保设备登录的是原购买所使用的 Apple 账号。

数据保存在哪里？

答题和下载记录保存在当前设备。删除 App 可能清除这些本地记录，但非消耗型购买仍可恢复。

需要进一步帮助？

请通过 App Store 产品页面中显示的“App 支持”方式联系开发者，并附上设备型号、iOS 版本和问题截图。

English

Help & Support

For Idiom Picture Quest

How do I start?

Tap Guess in the bottom tab bar, study the picture, and choose an answer from four Chinese idioms. There is no timer, so you can keep thinking or reveal the answer.

Why can I only access 10 puzzles?

The first 10 puzzles are free. A non-consumable purchase permanently unlocks the remaining 90 puzzles and can be restored with the same Apple Account.

How do I download images?

Tap the save icon in the top-right corner of a puzzle, or select several items in History. Image Download Tools also supports sets of five images, ready-made captions, and video creation.

Why can't I hear music?

Make sure Background Music is enabled under Me and increase the device media volume. If the app has just returned from the background, toggle Background Music off and on once.

How do I restore purchases?

Tap Restore Purchases on a purchase screen and make sure the device is signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Where is my data stored?

Answer and download history are stored on this device. Deleting the app may remove local records, while eligible non-consumable purchases can still be restored.

Need more help?

Contact the developer through the App Support method on the App Store product page. Include your device model, iOS version, and a screenshot of the issue.
