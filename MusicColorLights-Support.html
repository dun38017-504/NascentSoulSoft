Music Color Lights Support

Help with screen lights, music effects, the rear flashlight, and Music Color Lights Pro.

The flashlight does not turn on

Use a compatible iPhone; iPad models and the iOS Simulator may not provide a rear torch. Allow required system access, close other camera apps, and let the device cool if it is too warm.

Music effects do not react

For microphone mode, allow microphone access in iOS Settings and make sure sound is audible near the device. For local media, select a supported audio or video file and start playback inside the app.

Restore Pro

Open Music Color Lights, go to Settings, and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. You can manage or cancel a subscription from Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions on your device.

Full screen and locked lights

Full-screen display, Dynamic and Beams collections, Cosmic Sky, music-reactive lights, and beat flashlight require an active Pro subscription. Locked controls display a lock or PRO label.
