Frequently asked questions

  • Why are some photos missing? The current scan includes only locally available photos that appear to be camera captures. Videos, screenshots, suspected downloads, and iCloud-only originals are skipped. Limited Photos permission can also reduce what the app sees.
  • Will the app change my originals? Automatic organization only creates a local index and thumbnails. Deleting a Smart Album marks its photos in the app's recycle bin. Confirming deletion or emptying that bin requests deletion of originals from Photos and may sync to iCloud and other devices. Removing a recycle-bin marker restores ordinary browsing, not the deleted Smart Album.
  • How do I restore Smart Albums? Open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed into the Apple Account used for the purchase.
  • Does Smart Albums require a subscription? No. The current unlock is a one-time, non-consumable In-App Purchase.
  • Can I change Photos access? Yes. Use iOS Settings → Photo Chapters → Photos (wording may vary by iOS version).

常见问题

  • 为什么有些照片没有出现？ 当前只收录设备上可读取、判断为相机拍摄的照片。视频、截图、疑似下载图片和仅在 iCloud 保存的原片会被跳过；有限照片权限也会减少 App 可见范围。
  • App 会修改原片吗？ 自动整理不会移动、编辑或删除原片。删除智能相册只把照片标记到 App 回收站；在回收站确认“删除图库原片”或“清空回收站”才会请求真正删除，可能同步到 iCloud 和其他设备。移出回收站会恢复普通分类浏览，但不会恢复已删除的智能相册。
  • 如何恢复智能相册购买？ 使用购买时的 Apple 账户，在 App 的“设置”中点击“恢复购买”。
  • 智能相册需要订阅吗？ 不需要。当前为一次性、非消耗型应用内购买。
  • 如何修改照片权限？ 前往 iOS“设置 → 智能相册 → 照片”（不同系统版本的文字可能略有不同）。
