Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Your photos and videos stay in PrivacyCam's private storage on your Android device. The developer cannot view or recover your vault content.

1. Information and local storage

PrivacyCam does not require an account and does not send your photos, videos, device unlock credential, biometric information, precise location, contacts, or vault contents to us. New media is stored without additional file encryption in the app-private PrivateVault directory, excluded from Android backup/device transfer, and inaccessible to ordinary apps under Android's application sandbox. Older app versions may retain previously encrypted media for backward compatibility.

2. Camera and microphone

Camera permission is used to preview and capture photos or video. Microphone permission is used only to add audio to videos. This media is processed locally and is not provided to the advertising service.

3. Device unlock and biometrics

PrivacyCam does not create or store a separate vault PIN. Vault access uses Android's system credential screen, which may accept your fingerprint, face, pattern, device PIN, or password. PrivacyCam does not receive or store those credentials or biometric templates.

4. Advertising and third-party data

The Android version uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display interstitial advertising. Google and its partners may automatically process device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, ad interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices to provide, measure, secure, and—where permitted—personalize ads. This advertising data is separate from vault media; PrivacyCam does not send photos, videos, PINs, filenames, or decrypted content to AdMob.

Google's handling of information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising. Android may allow you to reset or delete the advertising ID and control ad privacy from device settings.

5. Consent and choices

Depending on your region, Google or the app may present consent and privacy choices before personalized advertising is requested. You may also use Android and Google account ad controls. If an advertisement cannot load, access to the vault is not blocked.

6. Data retention and deletion

Vault media remains until you delete an item or uninstall/clear the app. Uninstalling or clearing app data permanently removes local keys, settings, and media. Advertising providers retain their data according to their own policies and legal obligations.

7. Children

PrivacyCam is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information. Advertising is not intentionally configured based on vault contents.

8. Security and limitations

We use app-private storage and authenticated encryption, but no device or software is completely secure. Keep your device updated and protect your PIN. We cannot recover deleted files or forgotten encryption credentials.

9. Changes

We may revise this policy when features, laws, or service providers change. The date above identifies the current version.
