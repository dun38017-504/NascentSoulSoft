Privacy Policy

Photo GPS Scan  ·  Last updated: May 2026

🔒 Your data never leaves your device

Overview

Photo GPS Scan ("the App") is designed from the ground up to protect your privacy. All photo analysis and metadata processing happens entirely on your iPhone or iPad. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data or photos.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Photos or image files you scan
  • GPS coordinates or location data found in your photos
  • Device information, identifiers (IDFA, IDFV), or serial numbers
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to our servers
  • Any personally identifiable information

Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your Photos library solely to allow you to select a photo for scanning. Photos are read locally on your device and are never uploaded anywhere. When you save a cleaned copy, it is saved directly to your local Photos library.

In-App Purchases

Purchase transactions are processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We do not receive, handle, or store your payment information. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how purchase data is handled.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. There are no third-party services with access to your data.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since we collect no personal data from anyone, the App is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
