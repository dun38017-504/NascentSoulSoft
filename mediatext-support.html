Frequently asked questions

Which files are supported?

MediaText supports common audio and video formats that iOS can read, including MP3, M4A, WAV, MP4, and MOV.

Why did transcription fail?

Check Speech Recognition permission, network availability, free storage, and whether the file contains a clear audio track. Very long files, background noise, music, or overlapping speakers may reduce reliability.

Can I leave the app while it works?

iOS may suspend processing after the app enters the background. Keep MediaText open until the current transcription completes.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Settings in MediaText and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple ID used for the original purchase.

常见问题

支持哪些文件？

MediaText 支持 iOS 可读取的常见音视频格式，包括 MP3、M4A、WAV、MP4 和 MOV。

为什么转写失败？

请检查语音识别权限、网络、设备剩余空间，以及文件是否包含清晰音轨。超长文件、噪声、音乐或多人同时讲话可能降低稳定性。

转换时可以离开 App 吗？

App 进入后台后，iOS 可能暂停处理。请在本次转换完成前保持 MediaText 打开。

怎样恢复 Pro？

使用原购买 Apple ID 登录，在 MediaText“设置”中点击“恢复购买”。
