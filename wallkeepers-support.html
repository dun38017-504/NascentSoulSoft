Frequently asked questions

I paid but didn't receive my item.

Close the Game completely and open it again while connected to the internet. Unfinished purchases are delivered automatically when the Game starts. If the item still hasn't arrived after a few minutes, email us your Apple receipt (order ID only — never your password).

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I keep my progress on a new iPhone?

Sign in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on, on both devices. Progress is uploaded to iCloud automatically; you can also tap Settings → iCloud Save → Upload. On the new device, open the Game and choose to use the iCloud save when asked.

My progress is gone after reinstalling.

If iCloud was on before you deleted the Game, open it while signed in to the same Apple ID and wait a moment — it will offer to restore your iCloud save. Without iCloud, local progress cannot be recovered.

How does the Monthly Card work?

You get the Diamonds right away, then can claim a daily Diamond reward in the lobby (the Monthly button) for 30 days, and your max energy is raised. Buying again while active adds 30 more days. It never renews automatically.

How do I unlock Endless Mode and the leaderboard?

Clear Stage 3 to unlock Endless Mode (the Endless button on the right side of the lobby). Sign in to Game Center to post your best wave to the leaderboard.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Open Settings in the lobby (top-right gear) and switch Music, Sound or Haptics.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. The Game supports English and Simplified Chinese.

How can I stop my child from making purchases?

Use iOS Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases and set In-app Purchases to Don't Allow.

常见问题

付款成功了但没收到道具？

请彻底关闭游戏后在联网状态下重新打开，未完成的购买会在启动时自动补发。如果几分钟后仍未到账，请把苹果收据中的订单号发给我们（只需订单号，切勿发送密码）。

如何申请退款？

购买由苹果处理，请在 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请退款。

换新手机后怎么保留进度？

两台设备登录同一个 Apple ID 并开启 iCloud，进度会自动上传到 iCloud；也可以在游戏内点“设置 → iCloud 存档 → 上传”。在新设备上打开游戏，按提示选择使用 iCloud 存档即可。

重装后进度没了？

如果删除游戏前开启了 iCloud，用同一个 Apple ID 打开游戏稍等片刻，游戏会提示恢复 iCloud 存档。未开启 iCloud 的本地进度无法找回。

月卡怎么用？

购买后立即获得钻石，之后 30 天内每天可在大厅点“月卡”按钮领取钻石，并提高体力上限。有效期内再次购买会顺延 30 天。月卡不会自动续费。

无尽模式和排行榜怎么解锁？

通关第 3 关后解锁无尽模式（大厅右侧“无尽”按钮）。登录 Game Center 后，最好波数会自动上传到排行榜。

怎么关闭音乐、音效或震动？

点大厅右上角齿轮进入设置，即可开关音乐、音效和震动。

怎么切换语言？

设置 → 语言。游戏支持简体中文和英文。

怎么防止孩子误充值？

在 iOS“设置 → 屏幕使用时间 → 内容和隐私访问限制 → iTunes 与 App Store 购买项目”中，把“App 内购买项目”设为“不允许”。
