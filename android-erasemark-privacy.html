Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026 · Android version

EraseMark (Chinese name: 清除水印, package name: shop.nascentsoulsoft.erasemark) respects your privacy. This policy explains how the Android app handles information.

1. Photos and image processing

Photos you select, edit masks, OCR results, and processed images are handled locally on your device. EraseMark does not upload your photos or image-processing content to our servers.

2. Photo access

The app opens Android's system photo picker when you choose an image. Depending on your Android version, the system may grant access only to the items you select. You can review app permissions at any time in Android Settings.

3. Local history and deletion

Before-and-after images may be stored in the app's private local history. You can delete individual entries or clear the history inside the app. Uninstalling the app normally removes its private local data.

4. Advertising and third-party data

EraseMark uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banner and interstitial ads. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general location inferred from IP, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data to deliver, measure, limit, and protect ads. Depending on your region, consent choices and Google settings, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Google processes advertising data under its own policies. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can manage ad privacy controls at My Ad Center.

5. Consent and choices

Where required, the app may ask for advertising consent and provide privacy options. You may change applicable consent choices through the privacy options presented in the app or through your device and Google account settings.

6. Accounts, sale of data, and retention

EraseMark does not require an account and we do not sell your photos or image-processing content. We do not operate a server that stores those images. Advertising data retention is governed by Google's policies and your settings.

7. Children's privacy

EraseMark is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Security and policy changes

We use reasonable measures to protect app data, but no device or transmission method is completely secure. We may update this policy when features, providers, or legal requirements change and will show the revised effective date here.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, email dun38017@gmail.com or visit the Android support page.
