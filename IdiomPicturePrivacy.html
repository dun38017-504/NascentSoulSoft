成语小玩家隐私政策

更新日期：2026年9月23日

我们重视你的隐私。本政策说明“成语小玩家”如何处理信息。

本地数据

答题记录、学习进度、声音设置和下载记录保存在你的设备本地。App 不要求注册账号，也不会将这些记录上传到我们的服务器。

照片权限

只有在你主动保存题图或视频时，App 才会请求“添加到照片”权限。App 不读取你的照片图库。

购买信息

App 内购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理。我们不会获得你的银行卡或完整支付资料，仅根据 Apple 提供的购买凭证判断功能是否已经解锁。

网络与第三方服务

工具版不包含广告 SDK。App Store 购买、恢复购买及系统服务可能需要联网，并受 Apple 相应隐私政策约束。

未成年人

本应用面向成语学习用户，不主动收集儿童姓名、联系方式、精确位置或其他身份信息。监护人应指导未成年人使用购买功能。

数据管理

你可以在历史记录中清除本地答题记录，也可以通过删除 App 清除其余本地数据。已完成的非消耗型购买可通过 Apple 账号恢复。

联系我们

如有隐私问题，请通过 App Store 产品页面所列的支持方式联系开发者。

English

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

We value your privacy. This policy explains how Idiom Picture Quest handles information.

Local Data

Answer history, learning progress, audio preferences, and download history are stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account and does not upload these records to our servers.

Photo Access

The app requests add-only Photos access only when you choose to save a puzzle image or video. It does not read your photo library.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple StoreKit. We do not receive your card or complete payment details. The app only uses purchase information supplied by Apple to determine whether features are unlocked.

Network and Third Parties

This tool edition does not include an advertising SDK. App Store purchases, purchase restoration, and Apple system services may require internet access and are governed by Apple's applicable privacy policies.

Children

The app is intended for idiom learning and does not knowingly collect names, contact details, precise location, or other identifying information from children. Guardians should supervise minors when using purchase features.

Managing Data

You can clear local answer history inside the app. Deleting the app removes other locally stored data. Eligible non-consumable purchases can be restored through the same Apple Account.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support method shown on the App Store product page.
