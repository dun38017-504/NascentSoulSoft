Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

EraseMark (Chinese name: 清除水印) respects and protects your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Photos and processing data

Photos you select, edit masks, OCR results, and processed images are handled locally on your device. EraseMark does not upload this content to our servers.

2. Photo access

The app requests photo access only when you choose or save an image. You can change this permission at any time in iOS Settings.

3. Local history

Before-and-after images may be stored in the app's local history on your device. You can clear this history inside the app. Uninstalling the app also removes its local app data.

4. Data collection and sharing

The current version does not require an account, serve personalized advertising, sell personal data, or share your photos and processing content with third parties.

5. Children's privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. If we learn that information was collected unintentionally, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features or legal requirements change. The updated effective date will be shown on this page.

7. Contact us

For privacy questions, email dun38017@gmail.com or visit the EraseMark Support page.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 1 日

EraseMark（中文名“清除水印”）尊重并保护你的隐私。本政策说明 App 如何处理信息。

1. 图片与处理数据

你选择的图片、涂抹区域、OCR 识别结果和处理结果均在设备本地处理。EraseMark 不会将这些内容上传到我们的服务器。

2. 照片权限

App 仅在你主动选择或保存图片时请求照片权限。你可以随时在 iOS“设置”中修改权限。

3. 本地历史记录

处理前后的图片可保存在设备内的 App 历史记录中。你可以在 App 内清除历史记录；卸载 App 也会删除其本地数据。

4. 数据收集与共享

当前版本不要求注册账号，不投放个性化广告，也不出售或向第三方共享你的图片和处理内容。

5. 儿童隐私

本 App 不以收集儿童个人信息为目的。如发现误收集信息，我们会采取合理措施进行删除。

6. 政策更新

功能或法律要求发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策，并在本页面标明新的生效日期。

7. 联系我们

如对本政策有疑问，请发送邮件至 dun38017@gmail.com，或访问 EraseMark 使用支持页面。
