Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

This policy explains how Wall Keepers: Tower Defense (the "Game", Chinese name 萌兵守城) handles information. In short: we do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. The Game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

The App Store privacy label for this Game is "Data Not Collected".

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA), analytics SDKs, crash-reporting services or advertising networks, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

2. Game progress stored on your device

Your progress (levels, heroes, items, settings) is saved locally on your device. It never leaves your device except through iCloud as described below.

3. Apple services the Game uses

  • iCloud (Key-Value Storage). If you are signed in to iCloud, a copy of your game progress is stored in your own private iCloud account so you can continue on another device or after reinstalling. This data is held by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy, and is not accessible to us.
  • Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, your best Endless Mode score is submitted to a Game Center leaderboard. Your Game Center nickname and score are visible to other players as part of Apple's service. You can turn Game Center off in iOS Settings.
  • In-App Purchases. Payments are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your payment card, Apple ID or billing details; the Game only receives a confirmation that a purchase succeeded so it can deliver the item.

Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

4. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region). Parents can restrict in-app purchases with iOS Screen Time.

5. Deleting your data

Deleting the Game removes all local data. To also remove the iCloud copy, go to iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, or use Settings → Reset Save inside the Game while signed in to iCloud (the reset is synced to iCloud).

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy: dun38017@gmail.com

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 27 日

本政策说明《萌兵守城》（英文名 Wall Keepers: Tower Defense，以下简称"本游戏"）如何处理信息。简而言之：我们不收集、不存储、不出售、也不分享你的任何个人信息。本游戏没有账号系统、没有广告、没有数据统计、不做任何追踪。

本游戏在 App Store 的隐私标签为"未收集数据"。

一、我们不收集的信息

本游戏不会索取你的姓名、邮箱、手机号、位置、通讯录、照片或其他任何个人信息；不使用广告标识符（IDFA）、统计 SDK、崩溃上报或广告网络，也不会跨应用或网站追踪你。

二、保存在你设备上的游戏进度

你的游戏进度（关卡、英雄、道具、设置）保存在你自己的设备上，除下述 iCloud 同步外不会离开你的设备。

三、本游戏使用的苹果服务

  • iCloud（键值存储）：如果你已登录 iCloud，游戏进度的副本会保存在你本人的 iCloud 账户中，方便换机或重装后继续游戏。这些数据由苹果保管，适用苹果隐私政策，我们无法访问。
  • Game Center：如果你已登录 Game Center，无尽模式的最好成绩会提交到 Game Center 排行榜，你的 Game Center 昵称和分数会按苹果服务的规则对其他玩家可见。你可以在 iOS"设置"中关闭 Game Center。
  • App 内购买：付款完全由苹果处理。我们看不到、也不会收到你的银行卡、Apple ID 或账单信息，游戏只会收到"购买成功"的确认，用于发放道具。

苹果隐私政策：apple.com/cn/legal/privacy。

四、未成年人

本游戏适合所有年龄段，不会有意收集任何人（包括 14 岁以下儿童）的个人信息。家长可以通过 iOS"屏幕使用时间"限制 App 内购买。

五、删除数据

删除本游戏即会清除设备上的全部数据。如需同时删除 iCloud 中的副本，可在 iOS"设置 → 你的名字 → iCloud → 管理账户储存空间"中操作，或在登录 iCloud 的状态下于游戏内"设置 → 重置存档"（重置会同步到 iCloud）。

六、政策更新

如本政策有变更，我们会更新本页面及生效日期，重大变更也会在 App Store 更新说明中注明。
