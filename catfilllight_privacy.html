Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 16, 2026

English简体中文SupportTerms

Your photos and videos are stored privately inside the app. They are not added to the Apple Photos library unless a future version clearly asks for your permission.

1. Information we collect

Cute Cat Fill Light does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The current version contains no advertising, analytics SDK, or third-party tracking.

2. Camera and microphone

Camera access is used for live preview, photography, video, fill-light effects, and beauty processing. Microphone access is used only when you record video with sound. Processing takes place on your device. You can change either permission in iOS Settings at any time.

3. Photos, videos, and settings

Captured media is stored in the app's private local container and can be viewed through the in-app Private Album. App preferences, such as resolution, frame rate, fill color, and beauty settings, are stored locally on your device.

4. Retention and deletion

You may delete an item from the Private Album. Removing the app normally deletes its locally stored media and settings. Back up anything important before deleting the app; we cannot recover deleted local content.

5. Children

The app is not designed to collect information from children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above identifies the latest version. Questions may be sent to dun38017@gmail.com.
