Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my photos and videos stored?
They remain AES-256-GCM encrypted in PrivacyCam's app-private storage and are not added to Google Photos or the system gallery.
Why do I see an advertisement?
The Android app is supported by AdMob interstitial ads shown at natural transitions. Ads do not receive your vault photos, videos, PIN, or decrypted content. If an ad is unavailable, the vault still opens.
Why are camera and microphone permissions needed?
Camera access provides preview and capture. Microphone access records video audio. Neither permission is used to provide vault content to advertisers.
How is the vault unlocked?
PrivacyCam uses the phone's system unlock screen. Depending on your device settings, you can use a fingerprint, face, pattern, device PIN, or password. There is no separate PrivacyCam PIN.
Does PrivacyCam store my fingerprint or phone password?
No. Android performs authentication and only returns whether it succeeded. PrivacyCam never receives the credential or biometric template.
Why is the preview black?
Open Android Settings → Apps → PrivacyCam → Permissions and allow Camera. Allow Microphone for video audio, then reopen the app.
Is the vault backed up?
No. Private vault files are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently removes the vault.
Can ads access my private media?
No. AdMob is integrated for advertising but is never passed vault media, PINs, filenames, or decrypted content. See the Android Privacy Policy for advertising data details.
