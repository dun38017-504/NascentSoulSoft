Music Color Lights Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Music Color Lights handles information when you use the app.

Information we collect

Music Color Lights does not create user accounts and does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data for advertising or tracking.

Microphone and audio processing

If you grant microphone permission, the app analyzes sound level and rhythm in real time to animate lights. The app does not record, save, or upload microphone audio.

Imported audio and video

You may select a local audio or video file to drive music-reactive effects. Processing occurs on your device. The app does not upload the selected file. Your selection remains subject to the permissions and storage behavior provided by iOS.

Preferences and favorites

Settings, saved light combinations, and subscription test preferences used in development builds are stored locally on your device. Removing the app may remove this information.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Music Color Lights receives purchase entitlement information needed to unlock Pro features. Apple handles payment and related account information under its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The effective date above will identify the latest version.
