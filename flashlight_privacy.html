Privacy Policy

App: Flashlight & Color Light
Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

Flashlight & Color Light does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app operates entirely on your device.

2. Camera & Torch Access

The app uses the device camera solely to control the rear flashlight (torch). No images or video are captured, stored, or transmitted at any time.

3. Location Access

The compass feature uses Core Location to determine magnetic heading only. Location data is used in real time on-device and is never stored or shared.

4. In-App Purchases

Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store any payment information.

5. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

6. Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at: support@nascentsoulsoft.shop
