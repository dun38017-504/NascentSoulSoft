Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Photo Chapters organizes eligible photos on your iPhone or iPad. Your photo library stays under your control; the app does not upload your photos, videos, thumbnails, location information, or recognition results to Nascentsoul servers.

1. Who operates this app

Photo Chapters (Chinese name: 智能相册) is operated by Nascentsoul. Privacy questions can be sent to dun38017@gmail.com.

2. Photos access and local processing

With your permission, the app reads Photos-library items and available metadata such as capture date, location, and original filename. It creates local thumbnails, category labels, and Smart Album records. Automatic organization does not move, alter, or automatically delete originals. Deleting a Smart Album creates local recycle-bin markers. Only after explicit confirmation in the recycle bin does the app request deletion of originals through Photos. Deletions may sync through iCloud Photos to other devices; Photos normally places them in Recently Deleted. Current automatic scanning is limited to locally available camera photos; it skips videos, screenshots, images identified as downloads, and photos requiring an iCloud original download. Camera-origin detection is based on available metadata and is not infallible.

The app stores its indexes, thumbnails, labels, and album edits in its on-device storage. You can change or revoke Photos access in iOS Settings. Limited Photos access means the app can process only the items you selected.

3. iCloud and sharing

Automatic scanning does not request network downloads of iCloud originals. Apple Photos and iCloud may independently synchronize your library under your Apple settings. If you actively choose to share a photo, iOS may retrieve the original for that requested action; the destination and any subsequent processing are governed by the sharing method you choose.

4. Purchases and communications

The optional Smart Albums lifetime unlock is processed through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment information and purchase records. The app checks verified purchase entitlement to unlock the feature; Nascentsoul does not receive your payment card details through the app. If you email support, the email address and information you choose to include are used to answer your request. Please do not send private photos unless necessary and intended.

5. Retention, choices, and security

Local app data remains on the device until you remove it or delete the app, subject to any device backup settings you control. Deleting the app removes its local index and thumbnails but does not delete originals from the Photos library. We do not operate an app account, advertising SDK, or analytics upload for this version. Protect your device with its passcode and keep iOS updated.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. The updated date will appear here. For privacy requests or questions, email dun38017@gmail.com.

二、照片权限与本地处理

在你授权后，App 会读取照片库中的项目及可取得的拍摄日期、位置、原始文件名等信息，并在本机生成缩略图、分类标签和智能相册记录。自动整理不会移动、修改或自动删除系统照片库原片。删除智能相册仅建立 App 内回收站标记；只有你在回收站明确确认删除或清空后，App 才会通过系统照片框架请求删除原片。此操作可能同步至 iCloud 照片及其他设备，系统通常会将照片放入“最近删除”。当前自动扫描仅处理设备上可直接读取、判断为相机拍摄的照片；视频、截图、判断为下载来源的图片，以及需要下载 iCloud 原片的照片会被跳过。照片来源判断依赖可取得的元数据，不能保证完全准确。

索引、缩略图、标签和相册编辑结果保存在设备本地。你可在 iOS 设置中修改或撤销照片权限；若仅授权有限照片，App 只能处理你选择的项目。

三、iCloud 与分享

自动扫描不会请求通过网络下载 iCloud 原片。Apple 照片和 iCloud 可能依据你自己的系统设置同步照片库。如果你主动选择分享照片，iOS 可能为完成该操作读取原片；分享目的地及后续处理取决于你选择的分享方式。

四、购买与联系支持

可选的“智能相册永久解锁”由 Apple 应用内购买处理。Apple 管理付款信息与购买记录；App 只检查已验证的购买权益，不会取得你的银行卡资料。如果你主动发邮件寻求支持，我们会使用你提供的邮箱地址及内容来回复。除非确有必要且你愿意，请勿发送私人照片。

五、保存、选择与安全

本地 App 数据会保留在设备上，直到你删除相应数据或卸载 App；你控制的设备备份设置可能另有影响。卸载 App 会删除其本地索引和缩略图，但不会删除系统照片库原片。此版本没有 App 账户、广告 SDK 或分析数据上传功能。建议为设备设置密码并保持 iOS 更新。
